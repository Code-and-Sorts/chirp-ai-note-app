Using mouseless is as easy as one, two, click. First, tap command to bring up the overlay. Then press two keys to choose a cell. Then press space to click at the center of the cell. You can also press any of several keys to click at the corresponding sub-cell. In no time at all, you'll be faster at this than reaching four and using the mouse. When you need extra precision, you can nudge the cursor while holding the final key, then release to click. You can also get that precision with free mode, which is great for clicking and dragging, efficient scrolling, and it's even fun to make art with. There's a command for every mouse button and every action you might want to do. There's also global commands so you can maintain very precise position or movement while clicking with the keyboard. Or if you're like me, you'll simply prefer this over clicking with the laptop trackpad. So if you want to be more efficient, need relief from repetitive clicks, or want to be mouse-free on the couch. Mouseless is for you.
