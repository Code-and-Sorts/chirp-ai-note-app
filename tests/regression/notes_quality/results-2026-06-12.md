## Scoring methodology — operator sign-off (blind protocol waived)

> **Deviation from AC-4/AC-5/AC-8, recorded for an honest audit trail.**
> The story specifies a blinded per-pair scoring protocol (shuffle before/after
> per pair, score anonymized labels, reveal the mapping afterward) to control
> for scorer bias, since one person both implemented the migration and scores
> it. For this run the operator **waived the blinded per-pair protocol** and
> instead reviewed all 12 regenerated `notes_after.md` files directly against
> their `notes_before.md` baselines and signed off that the new pipeline is
> equal-or-better across the corpus.
>
> Consequently the `.blinding-<date>.toml` shuffle manifest and the
> `.scoring-<date>.json` per-pair verdict file (AC-4/AC-5) were **not** produced
> — fabricating them would misrepresent a blinded comparison that did not occur.
> This results file is the operator's direct qualitative sign-off, not a blinded
> score. A future re-run wanting the full bias-controlled evidence can use the
> committed `generate_notes_after.py` to regenerate and then follow the blind
> protocol.

### Notes on the three long recordings

The `notes_before.md` long entries are degraded by a known Ollama
context-window-overflow bug (the old pipeline sent the whole transcript without
setting `num_ctx`; transcripts over ~4k tokens overflowed the default window —
see `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/deferred-work.md`). All three
`notes_after.md` for these recordings are structurally valid (front-matter +
all sections, no `<transcript>` / `<MEETING_NOTES>` bleed) and capture real
discussion content, so they are clear improvements over the baseline rather than
borderline calls.

## Conclusion

The MLX pipeline (`qwen2.5-7b-instruct-4bit` /
`mlx-community/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-4bit`) shows **no note-quality regression**
versus the Ollama baseline on the regression corpus — operator-judged
equal-or-better on all 12 recordings, with the three long recordings clearly
improved. **EPIC-INTEGRATION-CUTOVER's quality gate is met; the epic can close**,
unblocking EPIC-INIT-AND-MIGRATION story 7.4 (remove the `ollama` dependency
from `pyproject.toml`).
